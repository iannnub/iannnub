## Projects

### [Puput LPG: LPG Distribution, Inventory & Accounting System](https://github.com/iannnub/puput-lpg)
**Next.js 15 (App Router, Server Actions), TypeScript, Supabase PostgreSQL 16 (RLS & RPC), React-PDF, ExcelJS**  
Live: [puput-lpg.vercel.app](https://puput-lpg.vercel.app)

Operational and accounting web app for an LPG distribution agency. It tracks gas cylinders across 4 physical states (`gudang_isi`, `gudang_kosong`, `gudang_bocor`, `dipinjam_mitra`). Client roles cannot mutate ledger or stock tables directly; all stock, receivable, cashflow, and payroll changes run inside 14 PostgreSQL RPC functions using `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` row locks to prevent race conditions. It also handles credit-limit locks with owner password overrides, FIFO employee loan deductions, A5 PDF invoices and payslips, and accrual vs. cash profit-and-loss reports.

### [SIMPRESMA: Faculty Achievement & SKS Conversion System](https://github.com/iannnub/simpresma-backend)
**Laravel 11, React 18, TypeScript, MySQL, GAPURA UNEJ SSO (OAuth 2.0 / OIDC), Zustand, TanStack Query, Telegram Bot API**  
Repos: [Backend API](https://github.com/iannnub/simpresma-backend) | [Frontend SPA](https://github.com/iannnub/simpresma-frontend)

Academic achievement and SKS credit conversion system for the Faculty of Computer Science at Universitas Jember. Students, lecturers, and staff log in through GAPURA UNEJ SSO (OAuth 2.0 and OpenID Connect) with state-token CSRF protection and automatic account provisioning. The app supports 5 roles (Mahasiswa, Dosen Verifikator, Tendik, Wakil Dekan I, Super Admin) with role switching, locks SKS conversion matrix snapshots at submission time, exports Excel reports, and sends status alerts through `@simpresma_unej_bot`. Tested with 82 PHPUnit tests (556 assertions) and 23 Vitest unit tests.

### [Platinum Project: Wedding & Event Decoration Management](https://github.com/iannnub/platinumproject-frontend)
**Laravel 12, Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, MySQL, Sanctum, Leaflet/OpenStreetMap, TanStack Query, Recharts**  
Live: [platinumproject-frontend.vercel.app](https://platinumproject-frontend.vercel.app) | Repos: [Frontend](https://github.com/iannnub/platinumproject-frontend) | [Backend](https://github.com/iannnub/platinumproject-backend)

Booking and financial management app for a wedding and event decoration vendor. Clients browse 13 decoration packages, pin their venue coordinates on an OpenStreetMap/Leaflet map, and book dates with WhatsApp admin routing. Admins manage schedules, down-payment and settlement logs, per-event operational expenses (RAB), 12-month cashflow charts, and net profit margins with PDF and Excel exports.

### [JemberTrip: AI Tourism Platform](https://github.com/iannnub/jembertrip)
**React, Vite, FastAPI, Python, PostgreSQL, ChromaDB, Scikit-Learn, LangChain**  
Live: [jembertrip.vercel.app](https://jembertrip.vercel.app) | Repos: [Frontend](https://github.com/iannnub/jembertrip) | [Backend](https://github.com/iannnub/jembertrip-backend)

Built a multilingual RAG chatbot (LangChain with HuggingFace and Groq LLMs) that answers Jember tourism questions in Indonesian, Javanese, and Madurese. The FastAPI backend logs clickstreams in PostgreSQL and runs vector search on ChromaDB. Its hybrid recommendation engine (Content-Based KNN Cosine Similarity + Collaborative Filtering) reached 56.55% Precision@6 and returned Top-N results in under 1 second. Over 1,500 people used the platform across 3 months during my undergraduate thesis research.

### [E-Voting OSIS SMPN 2 Balung](https://github.com/iannnub/evoting-smpn2balung)
**Next.js 14, React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, PHP 8.1+ Native MVC, MySQL (PDO)**
